# Data Deletion Instructions — Postiz Upload bot

**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

To request deletion of data processed by Postiz Upload bot, email [sartajprince1@gmail.com](mailto:sartajprince1@gmail.com) with the subject **Postiz Upload bot — delete my data**.

Include the WhatsApp sending number associated with your uploads and an approximate upload date or filename. State whether you want one upload removed, all your bot-related records removed, or your number removed from the approved-sender list to stop future uploads. Do not include passwords, API keys, access tokens, verification codes, identity documents, or another copy of the video unless the operator specifically explains why it is necessary.

The operator handles requests manually. The operator may ask for proportionate information to verify control of the relevant sending account, then promptly review and delete the relevant bot records, retained local video copies, and Postiz media under the operator’s control. Any operator-created backups and any separate social-media publication must also be reviewed. The operator will explain any limitation, lawful retention requirement, or copy outside the operator’s control and provide confirmation of the outcome.

Deleting a WhatsApp chat message, stopping the bot, or removing an app connection does not automatically remove previously copied files or local records. Meta/WhatsApp and other parties may retain their own copies under their policies. If your video was separately published on Instagram or another service, identify that post in your request so the operator can review it.

**This page provides deletion instructions, not an automated data-deletion callback.** The bot has no public deletion API, automatic deletion schedule, or guaranteed software-driven deletion deadline. Questions about these instructions can be sent to [sartajprince1@gmail.com](mailto:sartajprince1@gmail.com).
